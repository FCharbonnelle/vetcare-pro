## 2025-05-15 - [AI Assist Focus & State]
**Learning:** Combining immediate input focus (`autoFocus`) with reactive disabled states for primary actions significantly reduces cognitive load and anxiety in high-stakes contexts like pet health AI diagnostics.
**Action:** Always implement `autoFocus` on the primary input of task-oriented modals or screens, and ensure submission buttons provide both functional and visual feedback (opacity) based on input validity.
