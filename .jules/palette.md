## 2025-05-15 - Enhancing High-Stakes AI Interactions
**Learning:** In health-related AI diagnostics, combining `autoFocus` for immediate action with reactive disabled states (opacity feedback) reduces cognitive load and prevents invalid submissions during high-stakes context.
**Action:** Always include `autoFocus` on the primary input of creation modals and implement reactive visual feedback (e.g., reduced opacity) for submission button states to improve interaction flow and provide clear feedback on form validity.
